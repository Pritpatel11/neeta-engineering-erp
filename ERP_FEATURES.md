# BillGenie ERP Suite - Feature List

This document outlines the complete feature set of the BillGenie ERP Suite, categorized by modules.

## 1. 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- **Main Dashboard**: Real-time overview of business metrics, recent activities, and shortcuts.
- **Finance Dashboard**: Advanced financial metrics and area charts for revenue tracking.
- **Financial Year Toggle**: Global state switch to manage data across different financial years.

## 2. 🚚 Material Inward & Outward (Logistics)
- **Material Inward (CR Register)**: Track and manage incoming materials from vendors/contractors.
- **Create CR**: Form to log new material receipts.
- **Delivery Challan Management**: Complete challan lifecycle tracking and billing integration.
- **Create Challan**: Generate new delivery challans with dynamic rows.
- **Challan Preview**: Print-ready, formatted preview of challans.

## 3. 🏭 Manufacturing & Inventory
- **Inventory Balance**: Real-time tracking of current stock levels across divisions.
- **Pending/Remaining Material**: Track materials pending with contractors or in WIP (Work in Progress).
- **Production Entry**: Log daily production output.
- **Contractor Material Ledger**: Track material given to and received from specific contractors.

## 4. 📝 CRM & Sales (Pre-sales)
- **Website Enquiries**: Manage and track leads/enquiries coming from the website.
- **Quotation Management**: Track all sent and pending quotations.
- **Create Quotation**: Generate professional quotations for clients.
- **Quotation Preview**: Print-ready A4 preview for quotations.
- **Tender Management**: Track ongoing and upcoming tenders.

## 5. 💰 Finance & Billing (Post-sales)
- **GST Invoicing**: Generate and manage tax-compliant GST invoices.
- **Statement Management (MR)**: Manage monthly or project-based statements.
- **Create Statement**: Form to generate new financial statements.
- **Statement Preview/Register**: View and print statements.
- **Receipt Management**: Track payments received against invoices/challans.
- **Create Receipt**: Generate payment receipts.
- **Receipt Preview**: Print-ready receipt formatting.
- **Indemnity Bond**: Generate and print 1.5-inch top-margin formatted Indemnity Bonds on physical stamp papers.

## 6. ⚙️ Master Data & Settings
- **Master Data Management**: Manage core settings, company details, tax rates, and divisions.
- **Dynamic Company Switching**: Support for multiple companies (e.g., Neeta Engineering, K.C. Patel).

## 7. 🚀 Pro/Power User Features (Desktop-Grade)
- **IDE-Style Multi-Tab Interface**: Open multiple pages simultaneously (e.g., Create Invoice and Inventory) without losing form data.
- **Smooth Page Transitions**: Fluid Framer Motion animations when switching tabs.
- **Command Palette (Spotlight Search)**: Press `Ctrl + K` to open a global search bar and instantly jump to any page.
- **Quick Create Modal**: Press `Ctrl + N` to instantly open a grid of quick actions (New Invoice, New Challan, etc.).
- **Global Save Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl + S` to trigger a global save/draft event.
- **Smart Notifications**: Notification bell in the header that auto-polls and alerts for new website enquiries (with browser push notifications).
- **Print Anywhere**: Press `Ctrl + P` to trigger print on supported preview pages.
